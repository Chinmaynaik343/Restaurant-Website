Our Story

Tradition & Taste in Every Single Bite

Founded in 2012, Slice & Spice began with a simple vision: to bring genuine Italian pizza culture to your neighborhood. We source organic tomatoes from local farms, import authentic mozzarella, and ferment our dough for 48 hours to achieve that legendary light, crispy crust.

  • 100% Organic & Fresh Ingredients
  • Authentic Wood-Fired Brick Oven
  • Family Friendly Atmosphere & Private Dining
